# Ebby — Release Notes

What's new in Ebby, your AI-powered recovery companion. This page is a curated,
customer-facing summary of notable updates.

> Deliberately high-level. Detailed engineering release notes are maintained privately.

## 1.1.1

- Reliability and stability improvements.

## 1.1.0

- Smoother, more natural chat and voice conversations.
- Location-aware support, including a **Meeting Finder** to help you find nearby recovery meetings.
- Optional **wearable health integration** for more personalized insights.
- More personalized, context-aware guidance during conversations.
- Strengthened **security and privacy** protections, including multi-factor authentication for administrators.
- Broader platform and performance improvements under the hood.

## Earlier releases

Ebby's earlier releases established the core experience:

- AI-powered recovery conversations, in both text and voice.
- Personalized coping tools and guided exercises.
- Journaling with optional AI reflections.
- Daily check-ins and gentle, well-timed encouragement.
- Crisis support with quick access to emergency resources.
- Secure sign-in (email, Google, Apple) with biometric app lock.
- Privacy-first design — your data is encrypted and protected.
